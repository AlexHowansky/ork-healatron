Healatron

Treat Wounds healing calculator for Pathfinder 2e.

Healer

+1 item bonus to the check.

Medicine feats

The patient is immune for 10 minutes, not 1 hour. Needs expert Medicine.

Deal 1d8 slashing damage for +2 to the check; a success becomes a critical success.

A success becomes a critical success, if the patient hasn't regained HP from divine magic in the past 24 hours.

+5 HP at DC 20, +10 at DC 30 or +15 at DC 40 on a success.

Target

The target regains 5 more HP from a successful Treat Wounds.

The target regains HP equal to their level more from a successful Treat Wounds. Doesn't stack with Medic Dedication. Needs level 3.

The GM may raise the DC for the circumstances, such as treating outside in a storm or magically cursed wounds.

Results

On a success, keep treating for 1 hour to double the healing.
